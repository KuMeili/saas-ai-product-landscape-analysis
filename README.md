# saas-ai-product-landscape-analysis
Google Sheets analysis of Saas and AI product listings, industry verticals, and revenue models with charts including case study.

## Project overview
I analyzed a cleaned dataset of newly listed products using Google Sheets. The project explores product categories, revenue models, and reported revenue patterns.

## Business questions
1. How do SaaS builders currently structure their monetization, and what standard pricing architectures should we consider before expanding our Product-Led Growth (PLG) motion?
2. Where is builder activity most heavily concentrated across industry verticals, and how should this market density inform our R&D resource allocation?
3. What impact does founder commitment level have on top-line revenue performance, and how should it influence our portfolio operational risk management?

## Tools and skills
Google Sheets
Data cleaning and validation
Pivot tables
Charts and data visualization
Communicating findings and limitations
Gemini AI
